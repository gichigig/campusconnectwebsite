Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026

At IshinaDwelly ("we," "our," or "us"), your privacy is our priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the CampusConnect mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a better, more tailored experience to all our users. This includes:

  • Personal Information: Name, Email Address, and User IDs provided during registration.
  • Profile Data: School/Institution, Course, Year of Study, and short bios.
  • Media & Content: Photos, videos, and audio (including voice recordings) that you upload or share on the platform.
  • App Activity: Video views, likes, follows, and interactions with other users.

2. How We Use Your Information

We strictly use the collected data to operate the CampusConnect platform, including:

  • Managing your account and providing access to school-specific content.
  • Personalizing your feed through AI-driven video and audio analysis.
  • Facilitating social interactions (likes, follows, messaging).
  • Improving the overall functionality, safety, and security of the application.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share information when required by law or with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our platform securely (e.g., cloud hosting, analytics), under strict confidentiality agreements.


4. Data Security

All data collected by CampusConnect is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/SSL). Your passwords are securely hashed using bcrypt before being stored in our database.


5. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time. If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data, please visit our Account Deletion Page.
