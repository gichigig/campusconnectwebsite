Child Safety Standards Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

1. Our Commitment to Child Safety

CampusConnect is strictly committed to protecting minors. We have a zero-tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE). Any user found violating these terms will be immediately and permanently banned, and their information will be reported to the relevant national and international authorities, including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC).

2. Prohibited Content and Behaviors

Users are strictly prohibited from:

  • Uploading, sharing, or distributing any content that depicts, promotes, or normalizes the sexual exploitation or abuse of minors.
  • Engaging in grooming behaviors or attempting to contact minors for inappropriate purposes.
  • Sharing links to external sites that host CSAM or CSAE content.

3. In-App Reporting

CampusConnect empowers its users to help maintain a safe environment. Users can easily report inappropriate content or suspicious behavior directly within the app by clicking the "Report" button on any post, message, or user profile. Our moderation team reviews all reports with the highest priority.

4. Moderation and Enforcement

We employ both automated tools and human moderation to scan for and remove illicit content. Content identified as CSAM will be preserved as required by law and immediately forwarded to law enforcement agencies.
